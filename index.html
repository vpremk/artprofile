About

Art shaped by memory, ritual, and feeling.

Saffron & Silk is a contemporary art practice exploring the quiet drama of memory, handcraft, and color. Each work carries the rhythm of Indian folk traditions while speaking in a language that feels modern, intimate, and alive.

From expressive oils to narrative murals, the practice moves between raw emotion and crafted detail — where texture becomes story and color becomes mood.
